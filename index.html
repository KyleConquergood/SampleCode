Start each new shoe at zero. Add each visible card's value, including the dealer's hole card only if it is revealed. Divide the running count by decks remaining to get the true count.
Example: running count +6 with 2 decks remaining → true count +3. At +3, insurance can become profitable, unlike at a fresh shoe.

⚙ Table rules & settings

Settings can be changed between rounds; changing them starts a fresh shoe but keeps your bankroll. American hole-card/peek game. Split to 4 hands; split aces receive one card each, with no re-splitting aces.

Educational simulation using virtual credits only. The shoe and payouts are simulated, not connected to any casino. Basic strategy is total-dependent for 6–8 deck American hole-card games; some edge cases are composition-dependent. Hi-Lo decisions use the published Illustrious 18 and Fab 4 approximate indices, not an exact finite-deck solver, and indices vary with rules. Side-bet paytables vary by operator; the ones printed above are simulated here. High counts do not guarantee wins or a positive long-term expectation, especially with 6:5 payouts or shallow penetration. References: Basic strategy · Hi-Lo indices · Perfect Pairs.
